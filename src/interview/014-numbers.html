How to deploy a numberNot "it'll be fast" but "an indexed lookup is single-digit milliseconds, so at 40 requests a second that's well under one connection of concurrency — the database isn't the constraint here, the payment gateway's 800 ms is." Specific, checkable, and it moves the conversation to the real bottleneck.

Defaults that come up in follow-up questions

Interviewers who have run these systems ask about defaults, because the default is what most teams are actually running. Knowing the value, and why it matters, is a quick credibility signal.
