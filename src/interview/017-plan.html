A three-week plan

If the interview is booked, this order gets the most out of the atlas per hour. It assumes about ninety minutes on weekdays and a longer session at the weekend. With less time, shorten each week but keep the order: mechanisms first, then systems, then rehearsal.

Week 1 · Mechanisms

  • Spring Boot tab: Lifecycle, JPA and Internals. The proxy, the persistence context and transaction boundaries answer a third of any Spring deep-dive.
  • This page: Spring & Java and Security, answered out loud before you read the answers.
  • DSA: the orientation and complexity slides, then patterns 01–10, about two a day. Code the two "if you only drill two" problems for each.

The day beforeRe-read the numbers, the red flags and your five stories. Learn nothing new: new material the night before crowds out what you already know, and the interview rewards fluent recall of the basics far more than one extra pattern.
