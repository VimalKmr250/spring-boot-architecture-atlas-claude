Internal knowledge assistant: every employee's answer, filtered by what they may read

"Ask HR, IT and engineering docs anything" is the most requested internal AI feature, and the easiest to get dangerously wrong. Content lives in Confluence, SharePoint, Google Drive, Jira and Git, each with its own permissions. The assistant must answer only from documents the asking employee could open themselves, cite them, and stay current as pages change.

Permissions are the hard part

Copy each page's readers into chunk metadata as groups, keep them in sync on permission-change events, and drop anything you cannot map. If a source cannot expose permissions, only index its public spaces.

Freshness beats cleverness

An assistant that quotes last year's travel policy loses trust instantly. Event-driven updates, a nightly reconciliation that deletes vanished pages, and "last updated" shown with every citation.

The unexpected product

The log of unanswered questions is a map of missing documentation. Routing it to content owners improves the assistant and the knowledge base at the same time.
