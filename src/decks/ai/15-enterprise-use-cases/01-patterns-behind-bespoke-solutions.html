Four solutions enterprises build for themselves, and what they share

Beyond generic chat, the AI features enterprises fund are tied to their own data and processes: an assistant over internal knowledge, reports written from live numbers, documents checked against policy, and recommendations shaped by customer behaviour. Each gets a design slide and a code slide. They look different, but they are built from the same few parts, and the parts that make them safe are the same too.

The model never owns the facts

Numbers come from queries, policies from a versioned store, recommendations from your catalogue. The model reads, compares and writes; code checks what it wrote against the sources.

Identity flows end to end

The user's SSO identity scopes retrieval, tools and data access. A report job runs as a service identity with read-only access to exactly the tables it needs.

A person signs off where it matters

Compliance findings and outbound reports are drafts until approved. The system makes people faster, not absent, which is also what makes it approvable by risk and legal teams.

Everything is recorded

Inputs, retrieved sources, model and prompt versions, outputs and approvals, so any answer can be explained months later in an audit.
