When a RAG answer is wrong, find which stage failed before changing anything

A wrong answer has one of three causes: the right passage was not in the store, it was in the store but not retrieved, or it was retrieved but the model did not use it properly. Log the retrieved chunk ids and scores with every answer, and the first diagnostic question becomes a lookup instead of a guess.

Retrieval is usually the problem

In most RAG systems, the majority of bad answers trace back to retrieval and ingestion, not the model. Teams that swap models first spend money without fixing anything.

Make the evidence visible

Return the sources with every answer and record them with the conversation. A support agent who can click the cited passage fixes trust problems faster than any prompt change.

Say it in the interview"I debug RAG stage by stage: was it ingested, was it retrieved, was it used. Each stage has its own metric, recall at K for retrieval and faithfulness for generation, so I change the stage that is failing, not the model."
