RAG is two pipelines: one prepares your documents, one answers with them

Retrieval-augmented generation answers a question by first finding relevant passages in your own content and putting them in the prompt, so the model answers from evidence instead of memory. It is two systems with different shapes. Ingestion is a batch or event-driven ETL job. Retrieval runs on every question and has a latency budget. Most quality problems are ingestion problems that show up at retrieval time.

Why RAG and not a bigger context?

You cannot paste 40,000 documents into a prompt, and even when the relevant ones fit, sending them all costs money on every call and degrades recall. Retrieval sends the five passages that matter, with the user's permissions applied.

What it buys you

Answers from current, private content; citations a user can check; knowledge that updates when a document changes, with no retraining; and access control enforced at retrieval.
