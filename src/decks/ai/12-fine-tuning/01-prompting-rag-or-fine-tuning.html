Fine-tune for behaviour you cannot prompt; retrieve for knowledge you cannot train

Fine-tuning continues training a model on your examples so it adopts a behaviour by default: a format, a tone, a narrow classification skill, a domain's phrasing. It does not reliably teach facts, it is out of date the moment your data changes, and it adds a training pipeline to operate. Choose it only when prompting and retrieval have been measured and found wanting.

They combine

A tuned model that writes in your support style, answering from passages retrieved at run time, is a common end state. Fine-tuning shapes how it answers; RAG decides what it knows.

The data is the work

Hundreds to thousands of high-quality input/output pairs, reviewed, free of personal data, covering the edge cases. If you cannot assemble them, you cannot fine-tune, and the effort usually pays off better as an evaluation set.

Say it in the interview"RAG for knowledge, fine-tuning for behaviour. I'd fine-tune when a long prompt with many examples is the only thing holding quality up and the volume makes it expensive, or to distil a large model into a cheaper one for one task."
