Collectors that produce a string, a count or a statistic

These reduce a whole stream, or a whole group, to one value. On a plain stream most have a shorter terminal equivalent. Their real value is downstream of groupingBy, where no terminal operation can reach.

CollectorResult typeEmpty streamOn a plain stream, prefer
joining() · joining(", ") · joining(", ", "[", "]")String"", or "[]" with prefix and suffix—

Where they earn their keep: one value per group
String csv = orders.stream().map(Order::id).collect(Collectors.joining(","));

Map<String, Long> ordersPerCustomer = orders.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Order::customer, Collectors.counting()));

Map<String, Double> avgTotalPerStatus = orders.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Order::status,
                 Collectors.averagingDouble(Order::total)));

Map<String, DoubleSummaryStatistics> statsPerCustomer = orders.stream()
        .collect(Collectors.groupingBy(Order::customer,
                 Collectors.summarizingDouble(Order::total)));
MistakeWhat happens
Map<String, Integer> m = …counting()Does not compile: counting() yields Long. Declare Map<String, Long>, or use summingInt(x -> 1) for an Integer
joining() on a Stream<Integer>Does not compile: it needs CharSequence elements. map(String::valueOf) first
