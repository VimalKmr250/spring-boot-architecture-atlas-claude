Four questions before you type

  1. What is the output type? Map<String, Long>, List<String>, Optional<Employee>. Say it out loud; it picks the terminal operation for you.
  2. What is the shape? Filter, transform, group, rank or reduce, or a chain of two.
  3. Which collector produces that type? The cheat sheet at the end of this section maps question wording to idiom.
  4. What are the edge cases? Ties, empty input, nulls, duplicates. Name them before the interviewer does.
The data is chosen to have trapsEmeka and Farah tie at 81,000, so "the highest paid in Sales" has two correct people. Sales has four employees and the others fewer, so "the department with the most staff" has one answer. And no one in Support earns over 80,000, so a per-department filter has an empty group.
