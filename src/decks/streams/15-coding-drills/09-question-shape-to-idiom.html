The three follow-ups that always comeTies: "two people earn the most — which do you return?" Empty input: "what if the list is empty?", which is why the answers return Optional. Scale: "now it's 100 million rows" — push the grouping into SQL, stream from a cursor with try-with-resources, or keep a bounded heap instead of sorting. Having an answer ready for all three is what turns a correct solution into a strong one.
