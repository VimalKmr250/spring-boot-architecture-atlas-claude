Collector<T, A, R>: supplier, accumulator, combiner, finisher, characteristics, and two Collector.of factories. The characteristics are CONCURRENT, UNORDERED and IDENTITY_FINISH.

Gatherer<T, A, R>: initializer, integrator, combiner, finisher, andThen. Factories: of ×3, ofSequential ×4, and defaultInitializer, defaultCombiner, defaultFinisher. Nested types: Integrator (of, ofGreedy), Integrator.Greedy and Downstream (push, isRejecting).
