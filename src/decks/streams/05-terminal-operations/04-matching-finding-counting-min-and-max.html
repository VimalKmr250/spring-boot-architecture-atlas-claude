The small terminal operations, and their empty-stream answers

Seven terminal operations answer small questions: does any, all or none match; give me one; how many; which is smallest or largest. Most short-circuit. Their answers on an empty stream are where the bugs are.

One of each
boolean anyUnpaid  = orders.stream().anyMatch(o -> !"PAID".equals(o.status()));
boolean allShipped = orders.stream().allMatch(o -> "SHIPPED".equals(o.status()));
boolean noneEmpty  = orders.stream().noneMatch(o -> o.lines().isEmpty());

Optional<Order> firstBig = orders.stream().filter(o -> o.total() > 1_000).findFirst();
Optional<Order> anyBig   = orders.parallelStream().filter(o -> o.total() > 1_000).findAny();

long paid = orders.stream().filter(o -> "PAID".equals(o.status())).count();
Optional<Order> largest = orders.stream().max(Comparator.comparingDouble(Order::total));
