C4: four zoom levels, and only two of them are for everyone

Most architecture diagrams fail because they mix levels — a box labelled "Customers" next to a box labelled OrderRepositoryAdapter. C4's whole contribution is insisting you pick one altitude per picture.

The test for a good diagramHand it to someone who has never seen the system and ask them to explain it back. If they cannot say what each box does, which arrow carries the user's request, or where data is stored, the diagram is decoration. Adding a legend is usually a sign the encoding is doing too much work.
