RED, for every endpoint

Rate, Errors, Duration. Three dashboards panels per service that answer "is it working" without domain knowledge.

USE, for every resource

Utilisation, Saturation, Errors — connection pool, thread pool, disk, heap. This is where you look after RED says something is wrong.

Burn-rate alerts

Two windows: fast (1 h at 14× burn → page) and slow (6 h at 6× → ticket). It catches both the outage and the slow bleed, without paging on a single blip.

The four dashboards a team needsJourney (SLOs, customer-visible) · Service (RED per endpoint) · Dependencies (latency and error rate per downstream, circuit-breaker state) · Resources (USE). More than four and nobody opens any of them during an incident.
