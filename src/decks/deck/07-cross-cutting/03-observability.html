Observability: one trace id, end to end, across the async hop

Logs, metrics and traces are not three tools — they are three views joined by shared identifiers. The join is the whole value, and the place it usually breaks is the message queue.

application.yaml — the observability baseline
management:
  endpoints.web.exposure.include: health,info,prometheus,metrics
  endpoint.health.probes.enabled: true      # /readiness and /liveness, separately
  metrics.tags.application: ${spring.application.name}
  tracing.sampling.probability: 0.1
  otlp.tracing.endpoint: http://collector:4318/v1/traces

spring:
  kafka:
    template.observation-enabled: true      # producer spans
    listener.observation-enabled: true      # consumer spans — joins the trace
logging.pattern.correlation: "[%X{traceId:-},%X{spanId:-}] "
Cardinality killsA metric tagged with orderId or customerId creates millions of time series and takes your monitoring stack down. Tags are for bounded dimensions — endpoint, status, module, outcome. High-cardinality identifiers belong in logs and traces, joined by trace id.

Separate liveness (restart me) from readiness (send me traffic). Conflating them means a slow dependency triggers a restart loop across the whole fleet.
