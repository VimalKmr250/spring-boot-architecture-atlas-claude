Four layers, one direction of dependency

Ports and adapters, named in Spring terms. The rule is a single arrow direction: everything points inward at the domain, and the domain points at nothing.

Pragmatic dialA pure domain costs a mapping layer. For a CRUD-ish module (catalog admin), skip it: @Entity straight to the controller is fine and honest. Spend purity where the rules live — order, pricing, payment.
Test signalIf your domain test needs @SpringBootTest, the layering has already failed. Domain tests are new Order(...) and JUnit, nothing else.
