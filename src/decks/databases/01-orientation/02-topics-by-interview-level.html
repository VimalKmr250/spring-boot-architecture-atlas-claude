If you have one eveningRead the 30 questions, saying each answer aloud first. Then write three drills without looking: the Nth highest salary, customers with no orders, and top N per group. Those cover most of what a screening round asks.
If you have a weekOne section a day: SQL essentials, advanced SQL, indexes and plans, transactions, then MongoDB basics and modelling, then the drills. Run the queries yourself on the engine you will be interviewed on; the study plan has the setup.
