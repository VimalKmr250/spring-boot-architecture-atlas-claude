Splitwise: split rules compute shares, the ledger nets debts, the simplifier plans payments

The split types differ in how they turn a total into shares, and in what they validate, so each is its own record behind a sealed interface. Three of them are the same weighted split with different weights. The ledger keeps one netted debt per pair of people. The simplifier is a pure function from net balances to payments: a pure fabrication in GRASP terms, since no domain object owns it.

Strategy, sealedEach split rule is a strategy, with one method. It is sealed because the set of split types is part of the product and the parser must handle all of them. A new type (split by nights stayed) is a new record, one factory case, and a compile error until both are written.
Net per pair, or net per person?The ledger keeps "bo owes ana 400" per pair, so the app can show each friendship. Per-person net balances are derived from it, and are all the simplifier needs. Storing both would mean two sources of truth that can drift apart.
