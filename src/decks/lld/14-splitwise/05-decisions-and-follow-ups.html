Splitwise: the choices to defend, and why "fewest payments" is harder than it looks

The greedy simplifier never needs more than n − 1 payments, and it is what most answers use. It is not always optimal, though. The true minimum is found by splitting people into as many groups as possible whose balances sum to zero, which is NP-hard. Knowing that, and having a small counterexample ready, is the strongest answer to the last question of the round.

DecisionWhy, and the alternative
Paise in a longExact, fast, and the rounding rule is explicit. BigDecimal everywhere also works; a double never does.
One rounding functionEqual, share and percent splits all call byWeight, so they cannot disagree about the extra paisa.
Sealed split rulesA closed set the parser must handle; records validate themselves.
Debts netted per pairThe app shows one line per friend; the reverse direction is never stored.
Expenses keptThe ledger is derivable from the expense list, which makes edit and delete possible (rebuild) and gives an audit trail.
Greedy simplifierO(n log n), at most n − 1 payments, easy to explain. Exact search is exponential; use it only for small groups.
Greedy is not optimal: balances +6 +4 −4 −3 −3 greedy, biggest with biggest −4 pays +6: 4 +6 now +2 −3 pays +4: 3 +4 now +1 −3 pays +2: 2 −3 now −1 −1 pays +1: 1 4 payments optimal: two zero-sum groups {+6, −3, −3} → 2 payments {+4, −4} → 1 payment 3 payments
How to find the optimum, if askedThe number of payments is n minus the number of zero-sum groups, so maximise the groups. For up to about 20 people, a DP over subsets finds it: mark each subset whose balances sum to zero, and combine them. Say it, and say you would ship the greedy one.
Follow-upWhat changes
GroupsA Group owns its own Ledger; a user's overall balance is the sum across groups. Simplify within a group, never across groups, because people expect group debts to stay in the group.
Several payers on one expenseAn expense gets a payments map as well as a shares map; each person's position is paid minus share, recorded against the payers pro rata.
Edit or delete an expenseRecord a reversing expense (an accountant's approach, and an audit trail), or rebuild the ledger from the expense list.
Multiple currenciesMoney becomes a value object with a currency; ledgers are per currency, and conversion happens only at settlement, at a recorded rate.
Concurrent edits from phonesEach expense carries a client-generated id, so a retried upload is recorded once; the ledger's methods are already synchronized for one process. See idempotency.
Notify on new expenseThe ledger publishes ExpenseAdded; a notifier subscribes. The ledger stays free of email code.
