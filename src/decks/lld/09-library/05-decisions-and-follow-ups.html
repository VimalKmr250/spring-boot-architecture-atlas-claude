Library: the choices to defend, and the follow-ups to expect

The interviewer's follow-ups here are almost always about time: renewals, hold expiry, overdue reminders. Each one is a rule about dates, and the injected clock plus the one lock make every one of them straightforward to add and to test.

DecisionWhy, and the alternative
Book and Copy splitSearch and reserve work on titles; loans work on physical items. One class for both cannot represent two copies.
Waiting list per titleAny returned copy of the title serves the queue. A queue per copy would make a member wait for one copy while another sits on the shelf.
One lock on LibraryBorrow and return touch a copy, a member and a queue together. Finer locks invite deadlock for no measurable gain at library volumes.
FinePolicy as a lambdaThe rule is one function; the cap and the rate are its data. A class when fines depend on the member tier or the book.
Listener, not a notifier callThe domain raises an event; channels subscribe. Adding SMS changes no library code.
Exceptions for refusalsEach refusal has a reason the librarian's screen must show. A Result type with a reason is the alternative; say you would switch if refusals became part of normal flow.
The subtle bug to mentionA member on the waiting list who then borrows another copy of the same title from the shelf is still in the queue. When a copy comes back, it goes on hold for someone who already has the book. Fix it in borrow: remove the member from that title's waiting list. It is a one-line change, and spotting it yourself is worth more than the line.
Follow-upWhat changes
Renew a loanLibrary.renew(barcode): refused if the title has a waiting list or the renewal limit is reached; otherwise replace the Loan record with a later due date. A renewals count goes on Loan.
Holds expire after 3 daysA daily job asks each ON_HOLD copy whether holdUntil has passed, then gives it to the next in line or back to the shelf. The job takes the clock, so a test can move time forward.
Overdue remindersThe same daily job scans active loans due tomorrow or overdue, and publishes an event. The listeners do the sending.
Several branchesA Branch owns copies; search returns availability per branch. Transfers between branches make a copy IN_TRANSIT.
E-books with licence limitsA copy becomes a licence slot with no barcode. A sealed Item type with PhysicalCopy and Licence keeps the loan logic shared.
Full-text search at scaleMove search behind a CatalogueSearch interface; the in-memory filter becomes one implementation and a search engine another.
