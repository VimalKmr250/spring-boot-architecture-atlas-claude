Library: copies carry the status, members carry the limits, the library carries the rules between them

Status belongs to the copy, because it is the copy that is on loan or on hold. Loan limits belong to the member's tier. The rules that involve several objects (can this member take this copy, who gets a returned copy) live in the Library service, the one class that sees all of them.

ClassOwns the decision

MemberWhether they are allowed to borrow at all (limit, fines)
TierHow many loans, for how long

LibraryThe waiting list, and who a returned copy goes to
Why a listener, not an email callThe library raises "hold ready" and does not care how the member hears about it. Email, SMS and the app's inbox are subscribers. That is the observer pattern, and it keeps the notification vendor out of the domain.
