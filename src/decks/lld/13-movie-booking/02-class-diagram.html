Movie booking: the show owns who holds each seat, the booking owns its own lifecycle

Seat ownership lives in one concurrent map per show: seat to booking id. A seat is free when it has no entry, or when its booking has expired or been cancelled. The booking decides, under its own lock, whether it is still alive. The service is a facade that coordinates the two with pricing and payment.

Why the map stores ids, not bookingsThe claim map needs only an identity to compare and swap. ConcurrentHashMap.replace(seat, oldId, newId) compares with equals, so it succeeds only if the seat still belongs to the dead booking. Storing ids also keeps the show free of any reference to payments or users.
