Movie booking: many users, one seat map, and a hold that must expire

BookMyShow-style booking is the LLD problem where concurrency is the requirement, not a follow-up. Two people clicking the same seat at the same moment must not both get it. A user who selects seats and walks away must not block them forever. Payment takes time, and the hold can run out while it happens.

Functional requirements

  1. A show is a movie on a screen at a start time. Its seats have a category: regular, premium or recliner.
  2. A user selects one or more seats and gets a hold on all of them, or on none. A partial hold is never created.
  3. A hold lasts 8 minutes. After that the seats are free for anyone, without waiting for a cleanup job.
  4. The price is the category's base price, +25% on weekends, and must be changeable.
  5. Paying confirms the booking. If the hold expired while the payment was in progress, the payment is refunded and the booking fails.
  6. A user can cancel a hold before paying; the seats are released at once.
  7. Anyone can see the seat map: free, held or sold.

Questions to ask first

How many users on one show?
Hundreds at release time, all hitting the same seats. Design for contention.
Cities, theatres, search?
Catalogue browsing is out of scope; start from a show.
Cancelling a paid booking?
A follow-up, with a refund policy.
Payment provider?
Behind an interface; it can be slow or fail.
Seat rules (no single gaps)?
Not now. It is a good follow-up.

Nouns, and what they become

BookingService · facadeShowBooking Seat · recordMovie · recordSeatCategory · enumBookingStatus · enum PricingStrategyPaymentGateway

Not classes here: theatre, city, screen (the seat layout is all the show needs), user (an id), ticket (a confirmed booking).

Say the concurrency plan in the first ten minutes"Each seat of a show is claimed atomically; a multi-seat hold claims them in a fixed order and rolls back on the first conflict. Expiry is checked when someone wants the seat, so no job is needed for correctness." Interviewers want to hear this before the class diagram.
