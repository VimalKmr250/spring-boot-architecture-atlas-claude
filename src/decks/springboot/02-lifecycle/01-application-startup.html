From main() to "Started ShopApplication in 4.2s"

Ten phases. Knowing which one you are in tells you where a startup failure can be caught, where a property is readable, and why your @PostConstruct ran before the web server was listening.

Which hook to useApplicationReadyEvent for anything that must not run until the app is genuinely serving (warm caches, register with a discovery service). ApplicationRunner for one-off startup work. @PostConstruct only for initialising that bean — never for work that assumes other beans are ready, because ordering is not guaranteed.
