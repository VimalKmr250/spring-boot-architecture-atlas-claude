Actuator on its own port

management.server.port: 8081 keeps health and metrics off the public route entirely, so no gateway rule can accidentally expose /actuator/env. Standard practice in regulated environments.

Swap Tomcat deliberately

Exclude -tomcat and add -undertow or -jetty only with a benchmark in hand. On modern Boot with virtual threads enabled, the difference is rarely the bottleneck.

-web vs -webflux

Pick one. Both on the classpath makes Boot start as a servlet app and quietly ignore your reactive assumptions. Since Boot 3.2's virtual threads, blocking MVC scales far enough that WebFlux needs a specific streaming reason.
