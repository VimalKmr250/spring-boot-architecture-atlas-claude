Startup: what each technique actually buys

Startup matters for rolling deploys, autoscaling responsiveness and how quickly you can roll back — not usually for cost. Measure before choosing, because the expensive options buy the least in most enterprise services.

Honest framing for an interview"For a long-running service, 5 seconds of startup costs nothing at steady state. It matters for rolling deploys and for how fast we can roll back. I would trim the classpath, enable CDS, measure, and consider native only if we needed scale-to-zero." That shows judgement; "we'd go native for performance" shows the opposite, since native is usually slower at peak throughput than a warmed-up JIT.
