Consumer groups: the unit of parallelism and of isolation

A group is both a scaling mechanism (partitions split across members) and an isolation boundary (each group has its own offsets). Confusing the two produces the two most common Kafka mistakes.

Group id namingName it for the logical application, not the instance or the environment: inventory-order-reservation. Putting a hostname or a version in it creates a brand-new group on every deploy, which starts from auto.offset.reset — either replaying everything or silently skipping the backlog.
