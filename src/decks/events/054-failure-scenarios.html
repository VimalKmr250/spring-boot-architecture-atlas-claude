Six failures, and what to do at 3am

Runbook material. Each of these has happened to every team running Kafka for more than a year.

The one that catches everyone"Events stopped arriving" and every Kafka dashboard is green — because the problem is upstream of Kafka entirely. The outbox relay crashed, or the Debezium connector is in FAILED state. Both are invisible from the broker's perspective, and both are why the producer-side metrics belong on the same dashboard as the consumer-side ones.
What to write down before you need itFor each critical flow: which topics, which consumer groups, who owns them, what the SLO is, how to replay safely, and what is not idempotent. One page. Link it from every alert on that flow. The value of a runbook is entirely in it existing before the incident.
