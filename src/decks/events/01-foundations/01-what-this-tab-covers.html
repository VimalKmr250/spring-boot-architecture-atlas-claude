The thread running through all of itEvent-driven architecture trades immediate consistency for independent availability. Every pattern here is a way of paying for that trade honestly — idempotency, outboxes, sagas, replay. Teams that adopt the tooling without paying for the trade end up with a distributed system that loses orders quietly.
