What to alert on in an event-driven system

The instinct is to alert on broker health. The things that actually hurt customers are further along, and they are all measurable.

Lag alone is a poor alertA lag of 50,000 is fine on a topic doing 20,000/s and a disaster on one doing 5/s. Alert on estimated time to drain — lag divided by consumption rate — which is dimensionally the thing you care about and comparable across every topic.
The two SLIs to put in front of the businessFlow completeness: of the orders placed in the last hour, what fraction reached a terminal state. Flow latency: p99 time from order placed to confirmed. Both are in the product's language, both are measurable from events you already have, and both catch problems that every technical dashboard shows as green.
