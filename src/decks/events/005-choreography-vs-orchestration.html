Choreography vs orchestration

Two ways to get a multi-step business process done with events. The choice is usually made by accident and regretted at step five.

The rule of fivePast about four steps, choreography stops being cheaper. The flow exists only in people's heads and in traces; onboarding takes weeks; and a change to the order of steps requires coordinating four teams who each own one arrow. For enterprise processes with money in them, orchestrate.
