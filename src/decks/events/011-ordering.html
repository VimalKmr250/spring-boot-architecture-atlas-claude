Ordering: what Kafka promises, and what breaks it

Kafka guarantees order within a partition. Everything people believe beyond that is something they must build themselves — and five ordinary decisions silently break even that.

Choose the key by ordering need

Key on the entity whose events must be sequential — orderId here. Keying on customerId orders everything for a customer but creates hot partitions for your biggest accounts.

Design for out-of-order anyway

Carry a version or business timestamp and ignore anything older than what you have applied. A consumer that tolerates disorder survives retries, replays and repartitioning — all of which will happen.

Over-provision partitions

Since adding them later is disruptive, start with more than you need — 12 or 24 rather than 3. Partitions are cheap up to the thousands per cluster; repartitioning a live topic is not.
