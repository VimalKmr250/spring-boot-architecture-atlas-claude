Producer settings that actually matter

Fifteen or so of the hundred-odd options carry almost all the behaviour. These are the ones worth knowing by heart and defending in a review.

retries is the wrong knobSince Kafka 2.1 the producer retries until delivery.timeout.ms expires, so setting retries=3 does far less than people expect. Tune the timeout to the latency your caller can tolerate and leave retries at its default.
Where a producer blocks, and why it mattersBuffer full → send() blocks up to max.block.ms on your request thread. With a 60-second default, a broker problem becomes a thread-pool exhaustion outage in your web tier. Drop it to a few seconds so you fail fast and can shed load deliberately.
