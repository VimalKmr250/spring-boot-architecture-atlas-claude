If you have one eveningRead the 30 questions, saying each answer aloud first. Then do two drills without looking: the immutable class and your own HashMap. Those two cover most of what a screening round asks.
If you have a weekOne section a day, in order: language, collections, concurrency (two days), the JVM, then pitfalls and drills. Run each program yourself and change one line to see what breaks; that is what makes the answer stick.
