Why a greedy walk failsChoosing the cheaper neighbour at each step gives 1 → 1 → 4 → 2 → 1 = 9 on this grid — it takes the cheap 1 at (1,0) and is then trapped in an expensive bottom row. The DP compares complete futures rather than immediate steps, which is exactly what greedy cannot do. Mentioning that counter-example is a fast way to justify the approach.
