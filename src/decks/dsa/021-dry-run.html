Container With Most Water — the cleanest demonstration of Rule B. Area is min(h[l], h[r]) × (r − l). Width falls by one every step no matter what, so the only lever is height, and the shorter side is the one that caps it.

Why discarding the shorter bar is safe — say this out loudSuppose h[l] < h[r]. Any container using l and some r' < r has height at most h[l] and width strictly less than r − l, so its area is strictly less than the one just measured. Every remaining pair involving l is therefore already beaten, and l can be dropped with nothing lost. This is the entire proof, and interviewers ask for it far more often than for the code.
