LC 1109 traced on three bookings over five flights

Bookings are [[1,2,10], [2,3,20], [2,5,25]] with n = 5, and flight labels are 1-indexed. Each booking touches exactly two cells of D, whatever its width. One prefix sweep at the end produces all five answers.

The two rises stacking at index 1Bookings [2,3,20] and [2,5,25] both start at flight 2, so D[1] accumulates 45. Nothing special is needed for that — += already composes. It is the reason the pattern handles overlapping intervals without any sorting or merging, which is what separates it from the interval-merging pattern.
