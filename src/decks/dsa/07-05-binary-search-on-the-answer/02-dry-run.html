LC 875 traced on piles = [3,6,7,11], h = 8

Koko eats at k bananas per hour; each pile takes ceil(pile / k) hours because she never moves on mid-pile. Find the smallest k finishing within 8 hours. The answer range is [1, 11] — one banana per hour at worst, and the largest pile at best.

Setting the bounds correctlylo = 1, because a speed of zero never finishes. hi = max(piles), because eating faster than the biggest pile cannot beat one hour per pile — and with h ≥ piles.length guaranteed by the statement, that speed always works. Picking hi = sum(piles) also terminates correctly and costs only a handful of extra iterations; picking hi too small is the error that silently returns a wrong answer.
