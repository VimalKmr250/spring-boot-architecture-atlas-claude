Why this is optimal — the exchange argumentEvery index in band k is reachable in exactly k jumps and no fewer. When the scan reaches the band's edge it has seen every index in that band, so farthest is the true limit of band k+1. No alternative choice inside band k could reach further, because the scan already considered all of them. This is breadth-first search over layers, so the first band containing the target gives the minimum — and BFS optimality is the proof.
