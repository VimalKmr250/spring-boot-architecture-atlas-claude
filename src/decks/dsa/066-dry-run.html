Why the root is the answer, not the largestThe heap holds the k largest values, and its root is the smallest of those k. The smallest of the top four is precisely the 4th largest overall. Reaching for Collections.max(heap) here returns the 1st largest instead — a mistake that passes when k = 1 and fails everywhere else.
