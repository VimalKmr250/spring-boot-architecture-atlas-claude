Why dp[i−2] + nums[i] and not dp[i−1] + nums[i]Taking house i forbids house i−1, so the best you can have brought forward is the answer from two positions back. That single index is the entire encoding of the adjacency constraint — which is what makes this the canonical 1D DP. If the constraint were "no two within three", the recurrence would reach back to dp[i−3] and nothing else would change.
