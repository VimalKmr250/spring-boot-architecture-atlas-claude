Metrics say something is wrong, traces say where, logs say why

Each signal answers a different question at a different cost. Metrics are cheap aggregates you can alert on. Traces follow one request across services and show where its time went. Logs hold the detail of individual events. Tie them together with one trace id, and an incident goes from an alert to a graph to a trace to a log line in a few clicks.

Costs to design for

  • Metric cardinality: a label per user id makes millions of series. Keep labels bounded (endpoint, status class, region).
  • Log volume: use structured JSON, sample debug logs, and set retention by tier. Log bills rival compute bills.
  • Trace sampling: keep 1–10% at random, plus every error and slow trace, decided at the tail.
