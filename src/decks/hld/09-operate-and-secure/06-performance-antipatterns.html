Ten performance antipatterns and the fix for each

The roadmap's list of performance antipatterns is worth knowing by name. Each is a design smell that works at small scale and fails under load, and each has a standard fix you have already met in this deck. In an interview, spotting one in your own design before the interviewer does is a strong signal.

How to use the listWalk your finished diagram looking for each one. Is anything called in a loop? That's chatty I/O. Is any hot read going to the database every time? No caching. Does one table hold everything? Monolithic persistence. Is there a synchronous call to a slow third party on the request path? Synchronous I/O, and probably a candidate for a queue. Saying "one risk here is chatty I/O between the feed and profile services, so I'd batch those lookups" shows seniority.
