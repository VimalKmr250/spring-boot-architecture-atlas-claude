Page on how fast the error budget is burning, not on raw thresholds

"Error rate above 1%" pages too often at night on low traffic, and too late for a slow leak. Burn-rate alerts ask a better question: at this rate, how soon will the month's error budget be gone? A fast burn pages within minutes. A slow burn that would still exhaust the budget pages within hours. Everything else becomes a ticket.

Alert on symptoms

Page on what users feel: error-budget burn on the key journeys, and synthetic checks failing. Causes such as high CPU, a full disk or consumer lag go to dashboards and tickets, unless they predict a symptom soon (a disk full within 4 hours).

Every page must be actionable

If the response is "wait and see", it isn't a page. Each alert links to a runbook: what it means, how to check, how to mitigate (roll back, fail over, shed load). Review noisy alerts weekly, and delete or fix them.

Asynchronous systems need their own SLOs

For queues and pipelines, "requests succeed" isn't enough. Add freshness and throughput SLOs: 99% of events processed within 60 s, with the dead-letter queue empty. Alert on the age of the oldest message.
