HLD draws the boxes and arrows; LLD fills in the boxes

It is the same system at three zoom levels. High-level design stops at the service boundary: which services exist, how they talk to each other, and where the data lives. Low-level design opens one service up and decides its classes, interfaces and schema.

ArtefactsArchitecture diagram, data-flow and sequence diagrams, capacity estimate, API contract, ADRsClass and sequence diagrams, DDL, interface signatures, pseudo-code

Where they meetThe API contract and the data model belong to both. HLD decides that an order service owns the orders and exposes POST /orders. LLD decides the Order aggregate, its invariants, and the columns in the table. In an HLD round, sketch both at the level of resources and fields, not classes and methods.
