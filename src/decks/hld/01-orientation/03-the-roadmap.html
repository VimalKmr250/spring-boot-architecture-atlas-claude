The roadmap, and where each stop lives in this deck

The roadmap.sh system design path runs from vocabulary to cloud patterns. The deck follows the same arc but groups the stops by building block, and it ends where interviews start: with worked designs. Each stop below is tagged with the section that teaches it.

Beyond the roadmap

§03
Back-of-the-envelope estimation: the numbers to know and a repeatable method.
§08
Reliability: timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, rate limiting, multi-region, consensus, unique IDs and clocks.
§09
Security: OAuth2/OIDC, tokens, securing the edge, and alerting on SLOs rather than on CPU.
§10
Architecture styles compared honestly, with a matrix for choosing between them.
§24
Wrap-up: a trade-offs cheat sheet, red flags, and an answer checklist.

A four-week pace

  • Week 1: §01–§04. Learn the vocabulary, estimation, and the edge.
  • Week 2: §05–§08. Data, caching, async and reliability, which is the core of the deck.
  • Week 3: §09–§11 and the first six designs. Do each design yourself before reading it.
  • Week 4: the last six designs, out loud and timed at 45 minutes, then §24.
