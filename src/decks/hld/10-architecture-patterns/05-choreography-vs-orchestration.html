For multi-step workflows, decide who knows the plan

A multi-service workflow such as "place an order" can be coordinated in two ways. In choreography, each service listens for events and knows its own next move, and the plan emerges from the reactions. In orchestration, one component, a saga orchestrator or workflow engine, holds the plan as an explicit state machine and tells each service what to do.

A rule of thumb that holds upChoreograph simple fan-out, where one event has many independent reactions and none depends on another. Orchestrate business workflows with an order, compensations or timeouts, such as checkout, onboarding or claims. Workflow engines (Temporal, AWS Step Functions, Camunda) give you durable orchestration without writing the state machine yourself.
