No style wins everywhere, so pick by the forces you actually have

Architecture styles aren't ranked; each trades some properties for others. Score them against what this system needs now: team size, how fast you must ship, how independently parts must deploy and scale, how strict the consistency is, and how much operational load you can carry. Then name the trigger that would make you change your mind.

A defensible default for most interview designs"I'd start with a modular monolith, or a handful of coarse services, with clear data ownership. I'd use events for fan-out to things like notifications, search and analytics, and managed or serverless components for glue. Then I'd extract a service when a specific force appears: a part with a very different scaling profile, a separate team, or a compliance boundary." Then name that force for the system in front of you.
