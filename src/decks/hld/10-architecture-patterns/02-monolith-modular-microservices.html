How much to split is a spectrum, and the middle is usually the right start

The real axis is not "one process or many". It is how strongly the boundaries between parts of the business are enforced, and what price you pay for that enforcement. A modular monolith enforces boundaries in code at almost no operational cost. Microservices enforce them with the network, which buys independent deploys and scaling, and charges you distributed-systems problems in return.
