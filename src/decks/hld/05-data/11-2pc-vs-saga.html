Across services, trade atomic commits for sagas that can undo

Once an operation spans several databases (orders, payments, inventory), no single transaction covers it. Two-phase commit makes the databases agree atomically, by holding locks until a coordinator decides. A saga runs a sequence of local transactions and, if a step fails, runs compensating steps that semantically undo the earlier ones.

Two-phase commitSaga
GuaranteeAtomic: all commit or noneEventually consistent: all complete, or compensations run
IsolationFull, with locks held across the protocolNone between steps; others may see PENDING states
AvailabilityBlocks if the coordinator or any participant is downEach step retries independently; a durable log drives progress
NeedsEvery participant supports XA / prepareIdempotent steps, a compensation for each, a saga state store
UseInside one database cluster (Spanner, CockroachDB do this internally)Across services and third parties (payments, shipping, email)
Order the steps to shrink the undoPut steps that can't be undone last. You can't un-send an email or un-ship a parcel. Do the reversible, likely-to-fail steps first: reserve stock before charging the card, and charge before shipping. Orchestrated sagas (a central coordinator) are easier to follow than choreographed ones once there are more than three steps.
