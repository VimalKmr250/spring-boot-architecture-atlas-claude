Fail-over and replication: keep a second copy ready to take over

There are two ways to survive losing a node. Keep a standby that takes over (active-passive), or have every node serve all the time (active-active). The first is simpler. The second uses all the hardware you pay for, but it needs a way to settle conflicting writes.

What goes wrong in fail-over

  • Split brain: a network blip makes both nodes think they're the primary. Prevent it with a quorum or a lease, and fence the old primary.
  • Lost writes: with async replication, writes the standby hadn't received are gone. That window is the RPO.
  • Flapping: too-sensitive health checks fail over back and forth. Add hysteresis.
  • Untested standby: a fail-over nobody has rehearsed usually fails. Run game days.
