"Consistent" is a spectrum: say which guarantee you need

A consistency model is a promise about what reads may return after a write. Stronger promises are simpler to reason about and cost more latency and availability. Most products need strong consistency on a few operations and one of the session guarantees everywhere else.

ModelGuaranteeTypical implementationUse it for
Strong (linearizable)Behaves like a single copy; reads see all completed writesSingle leader + sync replication, consensus (Raft/Paxos), quorum R+W>NBalances, stock at checkout, unique usernames, locks
CausalEffects are never seen before their causesVersion vectors, dependency trackingComment threads, chat (a reply never appears before its message)
Read-your-writesA session sees its own writesRead from the leader for a few seconds after a write, or send a version tokenProfile edits, posting a comment, settings
Monotonic readsA session never sees older data than it already sawSticky replica per user, or reads with a min-versionFeeds, inboxes, anything refreshed
EventualIf writes stop, replicas convergeAsync replication, anti-entropy, last-write-wins or CRDTsLike counts, view counts, DNS, search index, analytics
WeakBest effort; reads may miss writes entirelyFire-and-forget, in-memory cachesVoIP, live video frames, multiplayer positions
