Design a chat app: millions of open connections, and no lost or reordered messages

Think WhatsApp or Messenger: 1:1 and group chat, delivered in real time when the recipient is online and on reconnect when they aren't, in order, never lost, and synced across devices. The defining constraints are the huge number of persistent connections and the need to route each message to whichever server the recipient happens to be connected to.

Functional

  • 1:1 and group chats (groups ≤ 256 members).
  • Sent / delivered / read receipts, and online presence.
  • Offline delivery on reconnect, plus a push notification.
  • History synced across a user's devices; images via upload.

Non-functional

  • Delivery p99 < 300 ms when both users are online in-region.
  • Never lose an acknowledged message.
  • Messages ordered within a conversation.
  • Very high availability: chat is the product.

Scope it

  • End-to-end encryption: the server stores ciphertext only (mention it, don't design Signal).
  • Out of scope: voice and video calls, stories, payments.
  • Large broadcast channels (100k+) are a different fan-out problem. Flag it.
