Every knob trades one thing for another: consistency levels per request, compaction per table

The strength of this design is that it exposes its trade-offs as settings. Callers choose how many replicas to wait for, so they trade latency and availability against staleness on each request. Operators choose a compaction strategy per table, trading write amplification against read and space amplification. Close the interview by walking both dials.

Summarise"A leaderless ring. Consistent hashing with vnodes places keys on N zone-aware replicas, and any node coordinates with tunable W and R. Hinted handoff keeps writes available, vector clocks or LWW settle conflicts, and read repair plus Merkle anti-entropy converge replicas. Each node is an LSM engine with a commit log.
