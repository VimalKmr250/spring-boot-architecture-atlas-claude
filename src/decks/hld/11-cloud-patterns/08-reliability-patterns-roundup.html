Four more reliability patterns to have in your vocabulary

Retry, circuit breaker, bulkhead, throttling and deployment stamps each had a slide of their own. Four more round out the catalogue: expose health so something can act on it, keep configuration outside the build, serve users from active nodes everywhere, and pack workloads onto shared compute sensibly.

Geodes vs deployment stamps

Both deploy many copies of the stack. Stamps/cells partition users: each copy owns a subset of tenants, and a user always lands in their cell. Geodes are interchangeable: any copy serves any user, routed by proximity, with data replicated everywhere. Geodes give the lowest latency and survive the loss of a region. Cells give the tightest blast radius.

Consolidation vs isolation

Packing services onto shared nodes (Kubernetes) cuts cost and idle capacity, and it creates noisy-neighbour risk. Protect yourself with resource requests and limits, priority classes, and separate node pools for critical or bursty workloads. It's the bulkhead trade-off again, at the infrastructure level.
