Design an online store: two systems in one, browse at scale and checkout without mistakes

Shoppers search, browse product pages, fill a cart, check out and pay. The numbers split the problem in two. Browsing is a huge, read-heavy workload that tolerates slightly stale data. Checkout is small in volume but must never oversell stock or charge a card twice. Say this early, because it justifies every later choice.

Functional

  • Search and browse the catalog; product pages with price and availability.
  • Cart that survives across devices and sessions.
  • Checkout, payment, order status and history; cancellations and refunds.

Non-functional

  • Browse p99 < 200 ms and highly available; a slightly stale stock badge is acceptable.
  • Checkout: never oversell, never double-charge. Strong consistency where money and stock change.
  • Peaks of 10–20× at sales, which are planned, so scale ahead of time.

Clarify

  • Own stock only, or a marketplace with third-party sellers?
  • Payments through a provider (Stripe, Adyen)? Yes: never store card numbers, to stay out of most PCI scope.
  • Flash sales with contested stock? One region or several?
