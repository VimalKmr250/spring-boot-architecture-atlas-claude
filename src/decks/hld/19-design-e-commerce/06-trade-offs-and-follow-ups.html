Spend consistency only where money and stock change, and scale for the sale before it starts

The big trade-off is a spectrum, not a switch. Along the shopper's journey, staleness is fine at first (search, product pages) and unacceptable at the end (reserve, pay). The operational trade-off is timing: sales are planned, so capacity should be too. An autoscaler that reacts at 12:00:30 is too late.

Summarise"Browsing is a cached fan-out, allowed to be seconds stale. Checkout is an orchestrated saga: reserve with a guarded update and a TTL, authorise with an idempotency key, then confirm, and publish through an outbox. Flash sales add a waiting room and an atomic gate so that only winners reach the database."
