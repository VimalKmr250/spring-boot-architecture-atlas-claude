A cached read path for browsing, an orchestrated write path for checkout

Draw the two halves separately. Browsing fans out from a page BFF to catalog, pricing, availability and search, each heavily cached and allowed to be seconds stale. Checkout goes through an order service that orchestrates reserve, then pay, then confirm, against transactional stores, and publishes what happened through an outbox.

Product page = a BFF fan-out

One request for the page becomes parallel calls to catalog, price, availability, reviews and recommendations, each with a tight timeout and a fallback. A missing section is hidden, not a 500. Cache the assembled, non-personal part of the page at the CDN for a short time.

Search is a projection

Catalog changes flow through CDC or events into OpenSearch, typically within seconds. Search results can show a stale price or stock badge, which is why checkout re-reads the authoritative values before charging.

Why reserve before pay

Reserving is cheap and reversible: releasing a hold costs nothing. Charging a card is external and harder to undo. Doing the reversible step first means most failures need no refund, only a released hold.
