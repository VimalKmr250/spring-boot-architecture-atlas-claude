Deep dive 1: a guarded update for everyday traffic, a gate in front of it for a flash sale

Overselling happens when two buyers read "1 left" and both decrement it. The fix is to make check-and-decrement one atomic statement and to hold stock with an expiry rather than subtracting it. That covers normal traffic. A flash sale turns one SKU row into a hotspot, so you add a cheap atomic gate in front of the database and let only the winners through.

No Redis? Split the row

Spread a hot SKU's stock across N bucket rows (for example 10 rows of 1,000). Each attempt tries a random bucket and falls back to others when it's empty. Contention drops about N-fold, and the guarded update is still the thing preventing oversell.

Idempotent, one per customer

Put a unique constraint on (customer_id, sale_id) and an idempotency key on the purchase request, so double clicks, retries and scripts can't take two units. Add per-account and per-device limits at the edge for bots.

Holds must expire

A sweeper, or a delayed message per hold, releases expired reservations. It decrements reserved, marks the row released and returns the token to the gate, all idempotently. Without expiry, abandoned carts quietly make stock "sell out" while nothing is sold.
