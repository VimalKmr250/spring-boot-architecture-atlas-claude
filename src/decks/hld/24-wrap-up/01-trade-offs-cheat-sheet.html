Every design decision is one of about a dozen trade-offs, so name it out loud

Interviewers listen for trade-off language more than for any particular technology. When you make a choice, say which side of which trade-off you picked and why the requirements favour it. The right-hand column points to where each one was decisive in the worked designs.

Trade-offLean left when…Lean right when…Decisive in
Consistency ↔ availabilityMoney, stock, uniqueness: a wrong answer costs more than no answer.Feeds, carts, presence: a stale answer beats an error.E-commerce, KV store
Latency ↔ consistency (PACELC)Readers must see their own and others' latest writes.p99 matters more than freshness; replicas far apart.KV store, typeahead
Latency ↔ throughputInteractive requests; small, immediate work.Batch and pipelines: batch, buffer and compress.Crawler, video
Sync ↔ asyncThe caller needs the result to continue.Slow, retryable or fan-out work: queue it and return 202.Notifications, video
Push ↔ pull (fan-out on write vs read)Few followers, many reads: precompute on write.Celebrities and huge fan-out: merge on read.News feed, chat
Normalise ↔ denormaliseWrite-heavy with integrity rules; ad-hoc queries.Read-heavy, known access patterns; one read per page.News feed, e-commerce
SQL ↔ NoSQLRelations, transactions, evolving queries.Huge scale, simple key access, flexible schema.URL shortener, KV store
Freshness ↔ cost (caching, TTLs)Data changes often and staleness is visible.Read-mostly data; cache aggressively at the edge.Typeahead, e-commerce
Exactly-once ↔ simplicityRarely worth it end to end across systems.At-least-once plus idempotency keys: the default.Notifications, checkout
Precompute ↔ compute on demandHot, bounded, predictable queries (top-k, feeds).Long tail, rarely asked, or too many combinations.Typeahead, video
Accuracy ↔ memory (probabilistic)Exact answers are required (billing).Approximate is fine: Bloom filters, HyperLogLog, count-min.Crawler, typeahead
Replication ↔ erasure codingHot data, fast repair, simple reads.Cold bulk data where raw storage dominates cost.File sync, video
Monolith ↔ microservicesSmall team, unclear boundaries, early product.Independent scaling, many teams, stable domains.Architecture patterns
The sentence to practise"I'm choosing X over Y because the requirement is Z; the cost is W, and if Z changes I'd revisit it." Say it once per major decision and the interviewer never has to ask "why?".
