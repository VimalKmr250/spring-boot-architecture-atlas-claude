What breaks at 10×, what fails, and what interviewers ask next

Finish by stress-testing your own design. Redirects must survive the loss of any single component, so each dependency on the read path needs a fallback. Growth pushes the design towards multi-region reads, but the write path can stay in one region, because links are immutable.

At 10× (200k redirects/s)Response

DB storage (30 TB)More shards; archive expired links; compress long URLs

Global latencyMore read regions; edge redirects for "no stats" links

Follow-ups to expect

  • Abuse: phishing and malware links. Scan on create (Safe Browsing API), rate-limit creates per user and IP, and add a takedown flag checked on redirect.
  • Expiry: a lazy check on redirect (410 Gone), plus a nightly sweeper that deletes expired rows and cache keys.
  • Custom domains: the tenant becomes part of the key, (domain, code).
  • Privacy: hash IPs in click events, and set retention limits on raw events.
