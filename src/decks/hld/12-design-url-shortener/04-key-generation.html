Deep dive 1: unique 7-character codes without a coordination bottleneck

This is the heart of the question. You need billions of unique, short, non-guessable codes, minted by many servers at once, with no global lock on the write path. There are three standard approaches, and the interviewer wants to hear why you rejected two of them.

The answer to give"Counter ranges. A small coordination service (a DB sequence or etcd) hands each Shorten instance a block of 1,000 ids, so the write path never waits on it. To stop people enumerating links, I pass the id through a reversible scramble, such as a keyed Feistel permutation over the 62⁷ space, before base62-encoding it. Codes look random and are still guaranteed unique. Custom aliases skip all this and use a conditional insert."
