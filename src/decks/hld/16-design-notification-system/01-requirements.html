Design a notification system: many producers, four channels, and users who must not be spammed

Every product team wants to notify users: order shipped, a login from a new device, "you have 3 new followers", a marketing campaign. A central notification platform gives them one API and handles channels (push, SMS, email, in-app), user preferences, templates, rate limits, retries and delivery tracking. The difficulties are wildly mixed urgency and volume, unreliable third-party providers, and not annoying people.

Functional

  • One API for all producers: send to a user, a list, or a segment, now or scheduled.
  • Channels: iOS/Android push, email, SMS, in-app inbox.
  • Templates with localisation; user preferences per category and channel.
  • Delivery status: sent, delivered, opened, failed.

Non-functional

  • Never lose a notification; never double-send an SMS or OTP.
  • Priority isolation between critical and bulk traffic.
  • Survive a provider outage (fail over to a secondary).
  • Per-user caps and quiet hours enforced centrally.

Clarify

  • Who owns the content? Producers pick a template; the platform renders it.
  • Can users unsubscribe per category? Yes, and that's a legal requirement for marketing.
  • Out of scope: campaign authoring UI and A/B testing.
