A pipeline of queues: accept, decide, render, deliver, track

Each stage has one job and a queue in front of it, so it can scale, retry and fail on its own. The key design move is keeping queues separate by priority at the front and by channel at the back. A campaign flooding email can't delay an OTP, and a slow SMS provider can't stall push notifications.

Router workers decide

For each (notification, user): Is it a duplicate? Is the user opted in for this category and channel? Is it quiet hours in their timezone? Are they over their cap? Which devices are live? Then render the template in the user's locale and emit one message per channel.

Channel workers deliver

Batch where providers allow it (FCM multicast, email batch APIs). Respect each provider's rate limits with a token bucket per provider. Retry transient errors, drop permanent ones (invalid token, hard bounce), and record the provider's message id for tracking.

Tracking closes the loop

Providers call back with delivered, bounced or complaint. The SDK and email pixels report opens. Hard bounces and invalid tokens feed back into the device and contact tables, which protects sender reputation and cuts cost.
