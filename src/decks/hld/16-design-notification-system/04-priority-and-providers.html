Deep dive 1: isolate by priority, and route around failing providers

Two things threaten delivery. Load you create yourself, when an 8-million-user campaign lands on the same workers as OTPs. And third parties you don't control, when an SMS provider degrades, rate-limits you or goes down. Separate lanes handle the first. Per-provider limiters, circuit breakers and a secondary provider handle the second.

Throttle bulk on purpose

Send campaigns at a fixed rate (say 20k/s). This protects provider quotas and sender reputation, and it protects your own backend: 8 M pushes in one minute means 2 M people opening the app in the same minute. Spread sends by time zone and "send at local 10 am".

Provider abstraction

Hide providers behind one interface per channel, with health scores, cost and region routing. Fail over automatically on errors or latency. For SMS, route by destination country to the cheapest healthy provider; the costs differ by 10× across providers.

Retries that don't duplicate

Send each attempt with the same idempotency or message id where the provider supports it. A timeout isn't a failure. Query the provider's status before resending an OTP, or users receive two codes and the older one no longer works.
