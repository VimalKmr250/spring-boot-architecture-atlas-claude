REST for public resources, gRPC between services, GraphQL for many-shaped clients

All three can build any API. They differ in who shapes the response, the client or the server, what goes over the wire (text JSON or binary protobuf), and how well caches, browsers and tooling understand them. Here is the same question asked three ways: user 42's name and last three orders.
