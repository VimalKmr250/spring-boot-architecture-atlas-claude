Deep dive 2: fresh enough for breaking news, balanced enough for "s"

A daily rebuild is too slow when something happens in the news: people type it within minutes. Rebuilding the whole trie every minute is too expensive. The answer is layers: a stable base, a periodic delta, and a small real-time trending layer, merged when the query is served. Separately, prefix traffic is very uneven, so shard boundaries follow measured load, not the alphabet.

Trending, cheaply

A streaming job counts query frequency per minute with a count-min sketch (small, approximate) and tracks heavy hitters per short prefix. It emits queries whose rate is far above their baseline. The overlay is tiny: a few thousand entries, replicated to every server.

Safety at every layer

Autocomplete can put words in users' mouths, so it's a trust and legal surface. The blocklist and classifier run in the builder and on the overlay, and a manual kill list is applied at serve time for emergencies, taking effect in seconds.

Personalisation as a blend

Keep a small per-user list of recent searches (on the device or in a KV store) and blend it with the global results: "your recent" first, then global. That needs no per-user trie, just a merge of two short lists.
