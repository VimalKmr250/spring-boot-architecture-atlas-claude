Memory is fast, the network is slower, and crossing an ocean is slowest

You don't need these to the nanosecond. You need the orders of magnitude, because they decide designs: why a cache in memory beats a database round trip, why chatty services hurt, and why a synchronous call across continents can't sit on a 100 ms path.
