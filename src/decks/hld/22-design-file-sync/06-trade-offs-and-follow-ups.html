Pay for durability with erasure codes, and treat big shared folders as hot keys

At exabyte scale, storage overhead dominates the budget: three full replicas cost more than twice the raw disk of an erasure-coded layout with better fault tolerance. On the metadata side, the sharp edge is fan-out. One busy team folder with tens of thousands of members turns every commit into tens of thousands of pokes, unless notifications are coalesced.

Smart / selective syncShow placeholders for cloud-only files and fetch blocks on open; sync only the selected folders to small disks.
EncryptionAt rest per block with keys in a KMS, TLS in transit. End-to-end encryption on the client disables server-side dedup, preview and search.
Version retentionKeep journal entries and blocks for 30–180 days by plan; restore is "commit an old block list as new".
MobileNo constant background sync: upload the camera roll on Wi-Fi and charging; fetch on demand.
Summarise"Clients chunk and hash files, upload only the blocks the server lacks, and commit a block list against a parent revision. Metadata is an append-only journal per namespace in sharded SQL, and devices sync from cursors after data-free pokes. Conflicts keep both copies. Blocks are content-addressed and deduplicated, replicated while hot and erasure-coded after that."
