Design video on demand: a batch pipeline to prepare every upload, and a CDN to deliver it

Creators upload videos, and viewers watch them on any device and network, starting quickly and without stalling. The estimate settles the architecture in two lines. Processing uploads is heavy but can take minutes, so it is an asynchronous pipeline. Viewing produces tens of terabits per second of egress, which only a CDN can deliver.

Functional

  • Upload large videos, resumable over flaky networks.
  • Process into streamable renditions, thumbnails and captions.
  • Stream on web, mobile and TV at a quality that suits the network.

Non-functional

  • Never lose an original: it is the only irreplaceable byte.
  • Playback highly available and fast to start; processing may take minutes.
  • Cost-aware: storage and egress dominate the bill, not compute.

Clarify

  • Video on demand only, or live too? VOD first; live is a follow-up.
  • DRM needed (studio content), or only signed URLs?
  • Global audience? Limits on upload size and duration?
