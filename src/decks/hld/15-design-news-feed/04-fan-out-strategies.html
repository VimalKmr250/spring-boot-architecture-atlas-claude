Deep dive 1: push on write, pull on read, or both

This is the question the interviewer is waiting for. Push (fan-out on write) does the work once per post per follower, so reads are one lookup. Pull (fan-out on read) does the work every time a feed opens. Follower counts are wildly skewed, so real systems use a hybrid: push for most authors, pull for the few with millions of followers.
