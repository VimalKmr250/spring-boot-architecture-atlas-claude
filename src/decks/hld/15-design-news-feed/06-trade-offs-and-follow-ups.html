Viral posts, degraded dependencies, and deletes that must stick

A feed has many dependencies: graph, cache, ranker, counters, media. The page should get worse gracefully rather than fail when any one of them struggles. The other classic stress is a single viral post that every feed hydrates at once.

Follow-upAnswer sketch
Delete or edit a postMark it deleted in the posts store. Filter tombstones at hydrate time (the feed holds only ids), and lazily remove it from caches.
UnfollowStop future fan-out, and filter that author at read until the cached entries age out.
Author sees own post nowMerge the author's own recent posts at read time (read-your-writes), without waiting for fan-out.
New user, empty feedCandidates come from recommendations and popular posts until the follow graph fills in.
The trade-off sentence"We trade write amplification and some staleness for fast, predictable reads. Push for the 99.9% of authors with modest follower counts, pull for the celebrity tail, skip inactive users, and treat the feed as ids hydrated at read time, so edits, deletes and counts stay correct without rewriting millions of feeds."
