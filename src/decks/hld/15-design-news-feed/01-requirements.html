Design a news feed: cheap reads for everyone, including followers of celebrities

Think of the Twitter/X home timeline, Instagram or Facebook. Users follow others and open a feed of their posts, newest or best first. It's read-heavy like the URL shortener, but each read gathers posts from hundreds of sources. So the central question is when to do that gathering: when a post is written, or when a feed is read.

Functional

  • Create posts (text plus media), follow and unfollow.
  • Home feed: posts from people you follow, ranked, with infinite scroll.
  • Like and comment counts shown on each post.

Non-functional

  • Feed load p99 < 200 ms. This is the product's front page.
  • New posts visible to followers within seconds (eventual is fine).
  • Authors see their own post immediately (read-your-writes).
  • Handle extreme follower skew: 10 followers to 100 M.

Clarify

  • Chronological or ranked? Ranked, but design so chronological is a special case.
  • Do we store the whole history? Feeds show the last ~800 items; older posts come from profile pages.
  • Out of scope: ads auction, notifications, and search.
