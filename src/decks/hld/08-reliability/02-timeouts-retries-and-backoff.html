Retry with exponential backoff and jitter, or your retries become the outage

Retries hide transient failures. But when a dependency is struggling, every client retrying on the same schedule multiplies its load exactly when it can least absorb it. Three rules keep retries helpful: back off exponentially, add random jitter, and cap retries with a budget.

def call_with_retry(op, attempts=3, base=0.1, cap=2.0):
    for n in range(attempts):
        try:
            return op(timeout=remaining_deadline())
        except (Timeout, ServiceUnavailable, ConnectionReset):   # transient only
            if n == attempts - 1 or not retry_budget.try_acquire():
                raise
            time.sleep(random.uniform(0, min(cap, base * 2 ** n)))
        # 4xx, validation errors, "insufficient funds": never retried

Rules that prevent retry storms

  • Retry only transient errors, and only idempotent operations (or ones carrying an idempotency key).
  • Retry at one layer. Three layers × 3 attempts = 27 calls per user click.
  • Keep a retry budget: retries may add at most 10% extra load, and once it's spent, fail fast.
  • Honour Retry-After and 429. The server knows better than your constant.
