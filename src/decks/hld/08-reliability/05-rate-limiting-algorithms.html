Four ways to count requests, each with a different view of bursts

A rate limiter answers one question per request: is this caller over their allowance? The algorithms differ in how much state they keep, how they treat a burst, and how they behave at window boundaries. Pick the one whose burst behaviour matches the contract: "100 per second, bursts to 200" is a token bucket in plain English.
