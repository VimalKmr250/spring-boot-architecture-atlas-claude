RPO is how much data you can lose; RTO is how long you can be down

Zones protect you from a data-centre failure. Regions protect you from a regional outage, a bad regional deploy or a natural disaster, at real cost in money and complexity. Size the answer from two numbers agreed with the business. The recovery point objective is the maximum window of lost writes. The recovery time objective is the maximum time until service is restored.
